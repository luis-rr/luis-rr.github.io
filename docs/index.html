Hi! I’m Luis,

I do neuroscience with computers.

I am currently an HFSP postdoctoral fellow in Alex Cayco-Gajic’s lab at the Group for Neural Theory, École Normale Supérieure, Paris.

Research.

I am interested in the dynamics of brain activity at the network level. In particular, how activity can propagate reliably and flexibly, and how multiple circuits interact.

During my PhD and first postdoc, I studied reptile brains, from cortical dynamics in turtles to multi-area coordination during lizard sleep, to extract general principles of brain function from an evolutionary perspective. My current postdoc explores how multi-area dynamics evolve during motor learning.

I develop computational analysis techniques and models that are heavily grounded in data. I'm driven by the collaborative and interdisciplinary nature of neuroscience.
